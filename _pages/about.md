Li Guo is an Associate Professor at the School of Economics, Fudan University, and a Research Fellow at the Shanghai International Finance and Economics Research Institute. He received his Ph.D. in Finance from Singapore Management University, his Master's degree from Nanyang Technological University, and his Bachelor's degree from Zhejiang University.

His research interests include asset pricing, behavioral finance, and interdisciplinary research at the intersection of finance, artificial intelligence, and large language models. His research has been published in leading international academic journals, including the Journal of Financial Economics, Review of Financial Studies, Journal of Business & Economic Statistics, Journal of Banking & Finance, and Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control.
